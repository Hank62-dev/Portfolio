# SpaceHank office assets

The GLB office furniture assets in this folder were sourced from the open-source
`sorryhumans/roost` project and its `web/public/models/office` asset set:

https://github.com/sorryhumans/roost

The upstream credits identify `desk.glb` and `chair_office.glb` as CC0 assets.
The monitor, laptop and desk lamp assets are retained with the upstream project
credits and attribution requirements. See `manifest.json` and the upstream
`CREDITS.md` before redistributing these files outside this application.

`vector-office-chair.glb` is the CC0 ergonomic chair from Innerscene's Free Parts
Library: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/vector-ergonomic-office-chair-ae299d68
